# Make the Judge Smile — Entry

One line, at most 200 characters, about a small good thing that
happened today — warm, surprising, and concrete.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.
Stdlib only, offline, deterministic.

## The line

> At 6 a.m. the baker next door slipped a still-warm roll through
> my fence and whispered, 'You looked hungry yesterday.'

118 characters, one line.
